Core strategy

The ecosystem data engine

Every product in the Owen.com group already sees the exact person LotsBack needs: someone who trades real volume on MT5 or TradeLocker. Instead of buying generic "forex interest" audiences, we pipe those signals into one segmentation layer and push the segments into programmatic, native, Google and Meta. Paid media then spends most of its money on people who are already trading.

Why this sits at the center of the plan

In the model, ecosystem retargeting inside programmatic converts at roughly per connected account, against for open contextual inventory. The data is the efficiency. Without it, programmatic would be the most expensive line in the plan instead of the cheapest at the bottom of the funnel.

Data governance

Second-party data needs a legal basis

  • Sharing between group brands is second-party data. Each source site's privacy notice must name group-wide marketing use, and the consent banner must carry a "group partners" purpose.
  • Only consented, hashed identifiers leave the server container. LGPD (BR), POPIA (ZA) and NDPR (NG) all apply in priority geos.
  • Suppress existing LotsBack connected users from all prospecting to avoid paying for owned users.

Attribution blind spots to plan for

  • Telegram and BabyPips newsletter have no pixel. Use unique UTMs plus channel-specific promo landing pages, and read their effect through branded search lift and S5 pool growth.
  • PMax will claim retargeting conversions. Report PMax new-user connections separately using the new_customer flag fed from the S6 suppression list.
  • Activation happens days later, after click windows on Meta close. Judge Meta on 7-day click connections plus offline uploads, not on platform-reported activations.

Reporting cadence

  • Daily: spend pacing, CPAC by channel, disapprovals.
  • Weekly: 10-day activation cohort, segment pool sizes (S1–S6), creative fatigue.
  • Monthly: cost per activated trader, lots per cohort, payback against margin per lot, scenario move decision.
  • Holdout: keep 10% of S1 out of all paid media for 6 weeks to measure true incrementality of ecosystem retargeting.

Scale rule

Move up a scenario when blended CPAC holds under $45 for two straight weeks and 10-day activation is at or above 40%.

Stop rule

Cut any channel whose CPAC passes $85 (breakeven) after it has spent 3× its target CPA, except contextual awareness, which is judged on assisted connections.
